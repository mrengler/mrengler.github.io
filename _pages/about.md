I lead technical safety at [Microsoft AI](https://microsoft.ai/)'s Superintelligence organization, where my team is responsible for safety evaluations, post-training, and deployment mitigations for frontier models and agents. Previously, I led AI safety at [Inflection AI](https://inflection.ai/the-new-inflection) and spent several years building machine learning systems for platform manipulation at Twitter and malware and fraud detection in the cybersecurity industry.

I write about AI safety, security, and trust at [No Failsafe](https://nofailsafe.substack.com/) and am the co-author of [Introduction to Generative AI](https://www.maggieengler.com/book/).

I currently live in Brooklyn, New York with my husband and daughter.
